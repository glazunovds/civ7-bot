## 0. A bug in our code found on the way (VERIFIED from data)

`hostileAircraftNear()` (xpl-autopilot-units.js:2743) counts any unit with `Domain === "DOMAIN_AIR"`. Two commanders are DOMAIN_AIR too:
- `UNIT_SQUADRON_COMMANDER` Domain="DOMAIN_AIR", CoreClass SUPPORT (units.xml(B):82), and it is even tagged `UNIT_CLASS_AIRCRAFT` (units.xml(B):185).
- `UNIT_AERODROME_COMMANDER` Domain="DOMAIN_AIR", CoreClass CIVILIAN, BaseMoves 0, StaticConstructibleUnit, ConstructibleType BUILDING_AIRFIELD (units.xml(B):84). Every Airfield grants one (see §2).

So every visible enemy Airfield within AIR_WATCH_RADIUS 10 (its static commander), and every Squadron Commander, counts as a "hostile aircraft" and drives QUEUED_INTERCEPTOR, with no plane anywhere. Fix: require `def.CoreClass === "CORE_CLASS_MILITARY"` (our `isSoldier`) and `!u.isCommanderUnit`. Also: never use the tag `UNIT_CLASS_AIRCRAFT` alone to mean "a plane" (the Squadron Commander has it); `UNIT_CLASS_INTERCEPTOR` / `UNIT_CLASS_GROUND_ATTACKER` / `UNIT_CLASS_BOMBER` are clean.

## 3. Interception

- **Default 3, Aerodrome +1:** "Intercept is a PASSIVE ability … intercept range, which is set to 3 tiles by default. Aerodrome extends this range by 1 for Fighters stationed inside" (Civilopedia_Concepts_Text.xml:1272); "continue to intercept any incoming aircraft as long as they remain alive" (:1275); one Fighter intercepts, with a bonus per other Fighter packed with it (:1245). VERIFIED (text). The 3 itself is in no XML (grep of GlobalParameters and all data: none) → exe.
- **It is an army (commander) property:** every modifier is `EFFECT_ARMY_ADJUST_AIR_INTERCEPTION_RANGE`:
  - Airfield: `MOD_AIRFIELD_INTERCEPT_RANGE`, collection ALL_UNITS, subject `UNIT_AERODROME_COMMANDER`, Amount 1 (constructibles-gameeffects.xml(M):7-14), registered as a GameModifier: "Airfield Modifier Applies to all Aerodrome Commanders instead of to the Buildings" (constructibles.xml(M):872-874).

Its text says "+1 Intercept Range for Fighter Units" (TechnologiesText.xml(M):181), but the data puts it on the commanders. Carriers are tagged SQUADRON_COMMANDER, so they get it too.
  - Promotion Squadron "Ace": +1 (`MOD_PROMOTION_SQUADRON_ACE`, unit-promotions-gameeffects.xml:952-954; text "+1 Interception range for packed Fighters", UnitPromotionsText.xml:852/858).
  - So: range = 3 + 1 (base is an Aerodrome) + 1 (Flight mastery researched, any air commander) + 1 (Ace), measured from the **base** tile. INFERRED formula (the 3 is text; the +1s are data).
- **Getter:** none in the UI JS (only `PreviewTextInterceptor` lines in the combat preview, §6). UNKNOWN; **probe P-AIR-3** dumps the keys of `Armies.get(id)` for an air commander's army (`for (k in army)` / `Object.getOwnPropertyNames(Object.getPrototypeOf(army))`) to find an interception-range property.

## Implications for our code (short)

1. Fix `hostileAircraftNear`: military aircraft only (CoreClass MILITARY, not `isCommanderUnit`); otherwise enemy Airfields alone ask for interceptors. Count only those whose base (own location, else the army's) is within reach of our settlements; a bomber's threat radius is its Range (8–12) from its base, not 10 from the plane.
2. Fighters protect only within 3 (+1 Aerodrome, +1 Flight mastery, +1 Ace) of their base; counting interceptors empire-wide (`unitCount(isInterceptor)`) overstates cover. Count fighters by base distance to the threatened settlements; train them in the Airfield city nearest the threat, else rebase (`UNITOPERATION_REBASE` Plots, within 2 × movement).
3. A plane can only be trained where an Airfield stands and its Aerodrome army has a free slot; read `Armies.get(aerodromeCmdr.armyId)`'s `combatUnitCapacity - unitCount` before queuing (probe P-AIR-2 for queued planes).
4. Ground attackers (`UNIT_CLASS_GROUND_ATTACKER`) use RangedCombat vs units; bombers Bombard vs districts; fighters are weak vs ground (RangedCombat 35–40). `airAttack` by any plane at a raider is legal; preview with `simulateAttackAsync` once P-AIR-4 confirms.
5. Probes P-AIR-1..4 above (UI.log only).
